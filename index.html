Questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is Watch Logbook?

Watch Logbook is a web-based application designed for watch collectors. It lets you catalog each watch in your collection with photos, technical details, service history, and notes. It functions as a progressive web app (PWA), meaning it works offline and can be installed on your device like a native application.

How does offline storage work?

All data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server. Your photos, watch details, and service records stay entirely on your device. This also means the app works without an internet connection after the first load.

Is it a one-time purchase or a subscription?

One-time purchase of $4.99. There is no subscription, no recurring fee, and no premium tier. You get every feature, including all future updates, for a single payment.

Can I export my collection data?

Yes. You can generate PDF documents of your collection, which is useful for insurance documentation, sharing with other collectors, or keeping a printed archive. Your data is always accessible and portable.

What if I switch devices?

Since data is stored locally, you would need to export your collection and re-import it on a new device. The app is designed around the principle of local-first data ownership, prioritizing privacy and offline access over cloud synchronization.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account creation, no email required, no login. You purchase access and start using the app immediately. Your identity and collection remain private.

Is this open source?

The project is available on GitHub. You can review the code, report issues, or contribute.
